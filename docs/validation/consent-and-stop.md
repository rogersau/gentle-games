# Consent, Assent, And Stopping

## Consent And Assent

Before starting, the facilitator explains in simple language:

> We are checking whether the game makes sense and feels comfortable. You can say no, take a break, change your mind, or stop at any time. Nothing bad happens if you stop. You do not need to talk, look at me, sit still, or finish.

The caregiver gives informed consent through the approved study process. Explain purpose, activities, foreseeable discomforts, optional recording, data handling, who can see notes, and withdrawal without consequences. Ask for permission separately for any recording or off-screen activity.

Ask the child for assent in their preferred communication form. Assent can be a word, gesture, AAC selection, sign, approach, or another clear signal. Do not treat silence, stillness, compliance, or lack of resistance as assent. Re-check at each new game, device, support level, recording, and off-screen activity.

## Immediate Stop Or Withdrawal

Stop the current activity immediately, remove pressure, and offer the child control when any of these occurs:

- The child says, signals, or communicates "stop", "no", "done", "away", or an equivalent.
- The child withdraws assent, turns away, closes the app, moves away, covers ears or eyes, or otherwise indicates "not this".
- The child shows escalating distress, shutdown, panic, pain, fatigue, overload, or unsafe movement.
- The caregiver or child asks for a break or stop.
- The facilitator cannot tell whether assent is still present.
- The device, sound, motion, light, touch demand, or social demand appears unsafe or intolerable.

Stop means: stop the task, silence or remove the stimulus, return the device or choice, offer a quiet break, and ask only whether the child wants to continue later. Do not persuade, bargain, cheerlead, re-explain, promise a reward, ask "just one more", or frame stopping as failure. A child may withdraw without giving a reason.

Document only the minimum event needed for safety and product change, such as `S-04: stopped after sound; sound muted; no further task`. Do not document a diagnosis, health detail, or identifying description.

## After Withdrawal

The child's withdrawal remains valid after a break. If they re-initiate, confirm assent again and restart with a lower-demand option. If they do not re-initiate, end the session. The facilitator reports a product finding, not a participant failure.
