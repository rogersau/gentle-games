# Release Checklist

Participant validation evidence for every item below is **Pending**. These are acceptance checks to run, not claims that they have passed. Mark an item complete only after real child-centred review, caregiver consent, child assent, facilitator notes, and an independent review of the evidence.

## Shared Gate For Every Issue

- [ ] A child can understand the first instruction without adult explanation, or the product makes the missing comprehension visible.
- [ ] The instruction can be replayed and used through the available text, visual, spoken, and control channels.
- [ ] The child can start, pause, stop, skip when relevant, and leave freely without persuasion or completion pressure.
- [ ] A new in-game example tests transfer without the facilitator giving the answer.
- [ ] Support starts at the lowest level and fades; hand-over-hand help and correction loops are not required.
- [ ] Touch, non-drag, keyboard, switch, screen-reader, and reduced-motion paths are checked where supported.
- [ ] Frustration, avoidance, spontaneous sensory setting changes, and comfort choices are recorded as product evidence.
- [ ] No pass criterion requires eye contact, stillness, speech, suppression of harmless self-regulation, speed, or correctness in sensory/creative play.
- [ ] No identifying, health, diagnostic, or unnecessary performance data is committed.
