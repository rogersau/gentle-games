About

Gentle Games is a free, open-source app with eight released activities. Sound, motion, visual presentation, and game-specific settings are adjustable. Children can stop or leave at any time, and pressure-free presentation is the default. The app is not therapy, diagnosis, or a promise of educational or developmental improvement.

Games

🧩

Memory Snap

Guided practice. Remember and find matching pictures on the current board. Board size, preview, spoken labels, and optional counters can be adjusted.

🎨

Drawing Pad

Creative play. Make free-form marks or choose an optional trail, copy, or prompt. Tools are labelled, undo is available, and no creative score is assigned.

✨

Glitter Fall

Sensory/regulation activity. Add, move, and clear visual particles. Motion, speed, density, colours, and sound can be changed or turned down.

🫧

Bubble Pop

Sensory/regulation activity. Activate bubbles and see the immediate effect in free play, or choose an optional exact-count activity. Scores and milestones can stay hidden.

🗂️

Category Match

Guided practice. Sort an item using the stated Food, Toys, or Clothes rule. The rule can be replayed, and tap input is available instead of dragging.

🎈

Keepy Uppy

Sensory/regulation activity. Track a balloon and choose when and how to lift it. Six input profiles, resting balloons, adjustable motion, and optional counters are available.

🌸

Breathing Garden

Sensory/regulation activity. Use, watch, pause, or leave an optional visual pacing rhythm. The visual cue and music can be turned off, and there is no breathing requirement.

🚂

Pattern Train

Guided practice. Recognise and continue the current repeating rule. Instructions, hints, models, skip, and tap-based answers are available.

Accessibility and Adjustable Presentation

  • Animations can be turned off at any time
  • Sound, motion, colours, and game-specific settings can be adjusted
  • Large touch targets for small fingers
  • Pressure-free defaults hide optional scores, counters, and milestones
  • Children can stop or leave without completing an activity
  • Caregiver practice summaries are optional, local-only, and not assessments

Privacy

Gentle Games respects your privacy. Optional telemetry is off until a caregiver enables it. Sentry receives scrubbed technical error data and PostHog receives screen navigation only. Guided answers, support classifications, scores, and session duration are never sent. Optional practice summaries and all app settings remain on the device.
